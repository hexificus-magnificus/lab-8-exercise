The best part to make into a separate component would be the
BasicTransform. This is important to many systems like drawing,
collisions, movement, etc., and needs to be easily accessible
to them.

The rendering system must know which sprites are "in front of"
other sprites since compositing like this is non-commutative.
A simple solution would be to add sprites in the order they
should be rendered and maintain that order in drawing, but
a more complex system may require each sprite to have a depth
value.
